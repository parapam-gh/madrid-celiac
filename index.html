Madrid Meat‑Free & Gluten‑Free

Places picked from our saved Google Maps lists, plus a few 100% gluten-free brunch spots, for friends who don't eat meat (fish and seafood are fine), don't drink alcohol and have celiac disease. Every place was checked as open on 15 October 2026.

What to say

Show this to the waiter
Soy celíaco / celíaca.I have celiac disease.
¿Tienen platos sin gluten, sin contaminación cruzada?Do you have gluten-free dishes with no cross-contamination?
No como carne, pero sí pescado y marisco.I don't eat meat, but I do eat fish and seafood.
¿Lleva jamón o caldo de carne?Does it have ham or meat stock in it?
Sin alcohol, por favor.No alcohol, please.
  • Alcohol-free beer (cerveza 0,0) usually contains gluten. Ask for agua con gas, a fresh juice (zumo natural) or mosto (grape juice) instead.
  • Fried fish is usually coated in wheat flour (rebozado, fritura, calamares). Order it a la plancha (grilled) instead.
  • Ham hides in vegetable dishes: menestra, beans, croquetas, even salads. Always ask.
  • The weekday set lunch (menú del día) is the cheapest way to eat well, but it is the hardest to adapt for a celiac. Order from the main menu.

Not safe for celiacs

The Lebanese places (pita, bulgur, shared fryers); most Korean and Chinese places (soy sauce and gochujang contain wheat); the pizzerias (shared ovens); beer-and-fry seafood bars (La Mina, El Boquerón, Charnela, FIDE); Puntarena, Barracuda MX and DeAtún (wheat tortillas, soy, fryers); Sen Omakase, Yugo The Bunker and Santoku (gluten in most dishes); Tampu (gluten traces across the menu).

Won't adapt or too meaty

Bistronómika says it can't adapt its menus for allergies. Tripea has no celiac options. Los Arroces de Segis serves a fixed menu built on bread and cured meats. La Paloma (game and offal) and O'Grove (cured meats, gulas) have little else.
